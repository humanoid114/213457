ABOUT BASE FIT STUDIO

BASE FIT is a friendly gym built for everyone, from first-time beginners to experienced athletes. Our studio has modern equipment, group classes and a supportive community that keeps you motivated.

Whether you want to lose weight, build muscle or simply feel better every day, we are here to help you reach your goals.

ABOUT MAX RANDY

Max Randy is the head coach at BASE FIT. With more than 10 years of training experience, he helps members build strength, improve fitness and stay consistent.

His classes are simple, challenging and fun, and he makes sure every member gets personal attention.
